# TDUValues.github.io
The TDUvalues Political Quiz measures individuals' internal orientation to politics, covering 16 Axes in four supercategories of political life.  These categories are as follows:

SOCIETY IS...
Function - Descriptive beliefs about how model-able and predictable society is
Cultivation - Mostly Descriptive beliefs about how much individuals need society, and by extension how much they ought to operate within it
Production - Descriptive beliefs about the level at which social change and development originates
Direction - Mostly Descriptive beliefs about the grand trajectory of civilization and its value-proposition

WE SHOULD BE...
Behaving - Mostly Prescriptive beliefs about whether social expectations should be explicit and codified or informal and intuitive, and by extension the nature of what people want in their social conduct
Dreaming - Mostly Descriptive beliefs about how completely artificial the status quo and broader society are, and by extension the right to challenge such
Striving - Mostly Prescriptive beliefs about the inherent nature and desired direction of social improvement, surplus distribution, and work morality in the face of technological and productivity gains
Risking - Mostly Descriptive beliefs about the value and consequences of experimentation and risk-taking in technological and social domains, and by extension whether such change should be pursued or suppressed.		

MY MOVEMENT SHOULD BE...
Recruiting - Descriptive beliefs about how movements most effectively recruit people and deepen their commitment
Cohering - Prescriptive beliefs about how much conformity a movement representing one's own beliefs should impose in recruitment and followings
Pursuing - Descriptive beliefs about how movements most effectively pursue external political demands and accumulate power.
Reviewing - Prescriptive beliefs about how readily a movement representing one's own beliefs should revise said beliefs and principles

LEADERS SHOULD BE...
Mandate - Prescriptive beliefs about how movement leaders representing one's own beliefs should govern society
Program - Prescriptive beliefs about how movement leaders representing one's own beliefs should change society, either through material or cultural vectors
Time - Mostly Prescriptive beliefs about the primary unit of time a system representing one's own beliefs should focus policies and actions around
Space - Mostly Prescriptive beliefs about the primary level of representation a system representing one's own beliefs should focus policies and actions around

SOCIETY IS… (Function,Cultivation,Production,Direction)
FUNCTION - Predictable or Unpredictable (Mechanistic->Cybernetic->Polycentric->Emergent->Inexplicable)
CULTIVATION - Assembly or Atrophy (Authoring->Shaping->Integrating->Accompanying->Constraining)
PRODUCTION - Run by Systems or Great Men (Systems->Institutions->Organizations->Groups->Individuals)
DIRECTION - Rising or Falling (Ascending->Progressing->Enduring->Regressing->Declining)

WE SHOULD BE…(Behaving,Dreaming,Striving,Risking)
BEHAVING - Explicitly or Implicitly (Codified->Signaled->Situational->Customary->Tacit)
DREAMING - Contingent or Inherent (Constructed->Controlled->Contested->Conventional->Convergent)
STRIVING - Accumulation or Leisure (Mobilize->Escalate->Optimize->Decompress->Minimize)
RISKING - Accelerate or Reinstate (Unleash->Encourage->Manage->Restrict->Suppress)

MY MOVEMENT SHOULD BE…(Recruiting,Cohering,Pursuing,Reviewing)
RECRUITING - Incentivize or Inculcate (Offering->Engaging->Convincing->Converting->Instilling)
COHERING - Expansive or Exclusive (Diversity->Integration->Assimilation->Uniformity->Purity)
PURSUING - Aiming Low or High (Minimalism->Incrementalism->Opportunism->Expansionism->Maximalism)
REVIEWING - Process-oriented or Principle-oriented (Referendum->Provisional Law->Constitution->Canon->Scripture)

LEADERS SHOULD BE…(Mandate,Program,Time,Space)
MANDATE - Servants or Rulers (Serving->Collaborating->Consulting->Teaching->Ruling)
PROGRAM - Converting or Administering (Evangelist->Shepherd->Teacher->Steward->Engineer)
TIME - Present or Future oriented (Days->Months->Years->Decades->Centuries)
SPACE - Locally or Globally focused (Local->Sectional->National->Civilizational->Universal)
